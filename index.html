Start Your Ride

Every rider starts somewhere. The nth Ride is here to help you choose the right motorcycle, gear up properly, build your skills, and make informed decisions along the way.

Your first motorcycle does not have to be your dream bike. It should be a bike that gives you the confidence to practice, learn, and build the skills that will set you up for your dream bike.

Before You Buy: Learn to Ride

Before shopping for your first motorcycle, start with a rider safety course. A beginner course gives you a controlled place to learn basic motorcycle controls, stopping, turning, and other fundamental riding skills before deciding what motorcycle is right for you.

In California, the California Motorcyclist Safety Program offers training for new riders and provides the motorcycle during the beginner course. That means you do not need to own your first bike before you start learning.
