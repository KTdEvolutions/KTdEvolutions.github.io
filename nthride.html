Your nth Ride

There is no single path through motorcycling. Some riders keep one motorcycle for years while others discover new styles of riding and motorcycles as their experience grows.

Your next motorcycle does not have to be a predictable step up from the one before it. More experience gives you a better understanding of what you enjoy, what you want to learn, and what you want your motorcycle to do.

Learn. Ride. Learn More. Ride Better. Repeat.

Rider development does not end when you become comfortable operating a motorcycle. Every ride gives you experience, but experience becomes more useful when you continue learning how to improve.

Additional rider training can introduce new techniques, correct habits you may have developed, and give you a controlled environment to practice skills beyond the basics. Practice what scares and makes us uncomfortable to prepare for emergency manuevers.

What Do You Want From Your Next Ride?

Before deciding what motorcycle comes next, think about what you want to do differently. Your next motorcycle may offer more performance, greater comfort, longer range, better off-road capability, different technology, or simply a completely different riding experience.

Moving to another motorcycle is not only about adding engine size or horsepower. A motorcycle that better matches the way you want to ride can be a meaningful next step even if it is not more powerful than the motorcycle you already own.

Build Skills Before You Need Them

The street is not always the best place to discover the limits of a new skill. Training courses and controlled practice can provide a better environment for working on techniques that may be difficult to practice safely during everyday riding.

As your experience increases, continued training can focus on areas such as cornering, braking, low-speed motorcycle control, or other skills that match the type of riding you want to pursue.

Try Something Different

Your nth motorcycle does not have to be another version of your previous motorcycle. A rider who started on a sport bike may discover a naked bike, cruiser, adventure bike, dual-sport, or another style that provides a completely different experience.

Riding different motorcycles can also teach you more about what you value. Ergonomics, weight, suspension, power delivery, wind protection, technology, and handling can feel very different from one motorcycle to another.

The Dream Bike

Many riders have a motorcycle they eventually want to own. There is nothing wrong with having a dream bike, but getting there does not have to happen all at once.

The motorcycles you ride along the way can help you develop the experience and skills to better understand and enjoy the motorcycle you wanted in the first place.

There Is Always Another Ride

Motorcycling gives you an almost endless number of directions to explore. A different motorcycle, another type of riding, a new road, or another training course can all give an experienced rider something new to learn.

Your 1st ride gets you started. Your nth ride is wherever you decide to go next.
